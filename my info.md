## My History (Chronological)

I started as a hobby Minecraft modder soon after discovering Minecraft. I created Minecraft servers, built Bukkit plugins, and helped create models for some popular mods. I also got into building.

I finished the School of Electrical Engineering in Belgrade and started studies at the University of Communications Technologies (vocational studies in Multimedia) in Belgrade, where I gained academic knowledge of web development systems, databases, and programming.

I started working as a graphic designer/web developer, which I still do today. I create brand design systems and logos, and I love helping local businesses.

I also started participating in Erasmus+ programmes and built strong partnerships.

In Minecraft work, I started as a builder, but quickly switched to 3D modeling.

In April 2018, I started working primarily for Shapescape as an Asset Creator.
In 2019, I was promoted to Asset Creation Branch Lead, overseeing artwork within the team and advising on projects.

In 2021, I briefly served as Art Director.

Later, I moved deeper into development-focused work, collaborating with teams like XP Games and Nitric Studios.

In 2022, I started working at Tsunami as a Developer and Artist, and soon after became Project Lead.

Since December 2025, I have also been collaborating on projects with Simply Brilliant.
I am also currently making my own add-ons.

I am an active collaborator in the Bedrock OSS community and regularly build tools for other Minecraft creators.
